The method

From messy to institutional — in the only order that works.

Written for CEOs and controllers alike: each stage locks before the next begins. That sequencing is why multinationals stay fast without breaking — and why retrofit projects fail when they start with dashboards.

Data foundation

Master data, ledger mapping, entity normalization. Nothing credible is built on inconsistent inputs.

02

Disciplined close

A three-day close cadence, substantiated balances, controls your auditors respect on sight.

03

Planning rhythm

Budgets that survive contact

Locked baselines, rolling reforecasts, driver-based models. Versions stay comparable forever.

04

Decision reporting

Executive packs, RAG governance, variance stories. Nine minutes to read, one meeting to decide.
